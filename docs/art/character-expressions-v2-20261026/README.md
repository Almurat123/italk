## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one individual expression exploration image for the iTalk voice assistant avatar, part of a coherent nine-image set.
Input image: style reference only, showing the current simple circle and capsule-eye design language. Create a NEW refined expression concept, not a copy of the contact sheet.
Output: ONE square image with ONE character only, NOT a grid, NOT multiple examples.
Scene: perfectly plain warm off-white #F7F7F5 background. One perfectly circular flat solid near-black #111113 body, diameter exactly about 62% of the square canvas, centered at x=50%, y=48%. Generous clean negative space.
Visual language: extremely clean crisp vector-like 2D geometry, premium restrained digital character design, expressive through just TWO warm-white #FAFAF7 eye shapes. Body interior is one completely uniform solid color.
Eyes: together occupy only about 32% of the body width and sit slightly above center; more naturally forward-facing than an extreme sideways stare. Use expressive rounded capsule strokes/soft arcs. No black pupils, no irises, no eye highlights, no brows. Eye geometry and placement alone carry the emotion.
Keep exactly the same circle size, position, background, body color, eye color and flat rendering across all nine concepts. Do not add limbs, hands, mouth, nose, eyebrows, teeth, ears, accessories, logos, text, numbers, watermarks, shadow, outlines, gradients, glossy materials, internal patterns, waves, textures, 3D lighting or environment.


## Expression prompts

### 01 自然

Calm, naturally attentive resting expression. Two softly rounded ivory capsule eyes of moderate height, subtly unequal, gently angled about 12 degrees, facing mostly toward the viewer with a slight three-quarter turn. Quiet, approachable presence, not a smile and not an intense stare.

### 02 倾听

Attentive listening expression, clearly distinct from rest: both ivory capsule eyes taller and more upright, eye pair leaning together about 8 degrees as if quietly inclining to hear. A slight height asymmetry suggests active attention. Open and engaged, never alarmed.

### 03 思考

Thoughtful expression: the pair of ivory capsule eyes glances upward and a little to the side, positioned higher on the circle; one eye slightly shorter and more tilted than the other, conveying a brief reflective pause. Keep the circular character visible, no loading dots.

### 04 好奇

Curious, inquisitive expression: pronounced asymmetry, one tall softly curved ivory eye and one shorter inclined ivory capsule eye, with a small attentive head-tilt implied solely by their arrangement. Read as playful curiosity, not anger, not suspicion.

### 05 惊讶

Surprised expression: two larger open ivory oval-capsule eyes, nearly upright, similar height, slightly farther apart, placed near the upper middle of the circle. A clean, gentle 'oh!' expression without any mouth, pupils, eyebrow or extra mark.

### 06 开心

Delighted expression: the two eyes become simple clean upward-curving smiling eye arches, like two inverted U curves with round ends, broad enough to read at avatar size. The eyes themselves express joy. Absolutely no smile mouth. No sparkle, confetti or decorative symbol.

### 07 安慰

Warm reassuring expression: two relaxed ivory eye strokes, short gently curved horizontal capsules with subtle asymmetry, at a soft slight tilt. The look conveys kind, attentive reassurance rather than sleep or sadness. Eyes remain recognizably open enough to engage the viewer.

### 08 困倦

Drowsy expression: two small horizontal ivory rounded slits, gently drooping and almost closed, evenly placed with a very slight tilt, conveying a peaceful sleepy pause. Minimal, legible rounded ends, absolutely no sleeping Z, pillow, mouth or other prop.

### 09 俏皮

Playful expression: one eye is a clean short horizontal wink with rounded ends, the other is a lively taller ivory diagonal capsule. A slight asymmetrical upward placement conveys a mischievous friendly wink. No mouth, eyebrows, pupils, hand or accessory.
